# Summer Outfit Fashion Editorial

A curated AI fashion prompt for Mixed Fashion · Summer fashion editorial and luxury campaign photography · Image.

## Prompt

Create an ultra-realistic, cinematic summer fashion editorial featuring a stylish young woman in a beautiful outdoor setting during golden hour. Capture a series of premium lifestyle photographs with an effortless, sun-drenched summer aesthetic.

1. SCENE & STYLING

Explore two complementary summer environments:

- Poolside aesthetic: A woman wearing an elegant, lightweight beige or cream layered outfit, with flowing semi-sheer fabric and minimal sandals. Capture her leaning naturally beside an empty swimming pool, surrounded by palm trees and warm Mediterranean-inspired architecture.
- Tennis court aesthetic: The same woman wearing a fitted ivory sleeveless top, relaxed translucent white track pants, sporty sneakers and oversized sunglasses. Position her casually beside a tennis court net, with one hand resting against the fence.

2. PHOTOGRAPHY & COMPOSITION

- Dramatic low-angle photography with perspective-rich framing.
- Natural, candid poses rather than stiff fashion-model positioning.
- Full-body compositions with interesting foreground elements.
- Beautifully controlled backlighting and atmospheric sun flares.
- Natural shadows, subtle reflections and realistic environmental details.
- Shallow depth of field with softly blurred backgrounds.

3. LIGHTING & COLOR GRADING

Warm late-afternoon sunlight, golden highlights, muted olive greens, soft beige tones, cinematic teal shadows and delicate film grain. Create a nostalgic, dreamy summer atmosphere with a premium analog-film aesthetic.

4. REALISM & QUALITY

Ultra-photorealistic skin texture, natural hair movement, realistic fabric transparency and folds, anatomically accurate proportions, authentic environmental lighting and professional fashion photography.

FINAL AESTHETIC: Luxury summer campaign meets nostalgic 35mm editorial photography. Effortlessly stylish, atmospheric, youthful and naturally cinematic.

FORMAT: Vertical 4:5 composition, high-resolution 4K detail, professional editorial photography. No text, logos, watermarks or artificial-looking effects.
